    How it works

    1. Upload your raw clips (and optional background music) and pick a template.
    2. Silences are trimmed, transitions are applied, and your template's style is used throughout.
    3. Captions are generated with Whisper and burned into the video (if enabled).
    4. The final video is exported in every format you selected and packaged for download.

    ⚡ Pro features

    • AI‑powered silence trimming
    • Cinematic transitions and colour grading
    • Auto‑synced captions with Whisper
    • Multi‑format export (1080p, 4K, vertical, square)
    • Background music ducking

    ⏳ Processing time

    This is the most compute‑intensive tool. Larger clips and multiple exports take longer. You'll be redirected to a live progress page automatically.
