Falls back automatically to FFmpeg if AI is unavailable or fails.

Capped at 15 minutes per video on a shared server. 4K and 120fps take noticeably longer to process.

How it works

  1. FFmpeg handles denoising, sharpening, color, and base scaling in one fast pass.
  2. If enabled, AI models (Real-ESRGAN, RIFE) upscale resolution and smooth motion beyond what filters alone can do.
  3. If AI is off or unavailable, FFmpeg's own scaling and motion interpolation fill in automatically.
  4. The original audio is re-attached and you get an enhanced MP4.
