Video Speech Translator

Translate the spoken audio of a video into another language with dubbed speech and optional subtitles.

Drag & drop a video here, or browse

Supported formats: .mp4 .mov .avi .mkv
Leave as "Auto-detect" for automatic language detection.

How it works

  1. Upload a video with spoken audio (any language, auto‑detected).
  2. The speech is transcribed and translated into your chosen target language.
  3. A new voice track is generated and dubbed onto the video, replacing the original audio.
  4. Optionally, matching subtitles are generated and made available for download.

✨ What you get

  • Translated video with dubbed speech
  • Optional .srt subtitles
  • High‑quality audio using ElevenLabs (fallback to gTTS)

⏳ Processing

Larger videos take longer – the job runs in the background. You'll be redirected to a live progress page automatically.
