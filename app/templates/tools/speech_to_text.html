Speech to Text

Upload an audio file (WAV, OGG, MP3, M4A, FLAC, MP4) to get a transcription.

Drag an audio file here, or browse

.wav, .ogg, .mp3, .m4a, .flac, .mp4

How it works

  1. We convert your audio to a WAV format.
  2. Google Speech Recognition transcribes the speech.
  3. You get a plain text transcript.
  4. Longer files are split into 60‑second chunks and combined.

Supported formats

MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC – and even MP4 videos (audio is extracted automatically).
